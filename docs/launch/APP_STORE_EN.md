# App Store – English (draft)

**Subtitle:** Auto-sort photos into albums

**Promotional text:** Your trips, places and screenshots in real albums – sorted on your iPhone, no upload, no subscription. Plus your personal travel atlas to share.

**Description:**
Thousands of photos and you can't find anything? [NAME] organizes your library into real albums and folders – right inside the Photos app, on all your devices.

TRIPS WITH DATES — [NAME] detects your trips and creates albums like “Tuscany Aug 3–14, 2024”.
COUNTRIES, CITIES, YOUR PLACES — photos land in countries and cities automatically. Places you visit often are suggested; you name them.
SCREENSHOTS, DOCUMENTS, TOPICS — orders, tickets, recipes, receipts, dogs, beaches or food, recognized on device.
YOUR ALBUMS STAY YOURS — existing albums are recognized and completed instead of duplicated. You review every suggestion first, and you can undo everything.
YOUR TRAVEL ATLAS — a world map that lights up every country you photographed in, ready for your story.
PRIVATE BY DESIGN — analysis runs entirely on your iPhone. No uploads, no account, no tracking. Originals are never deleted, changed or copied.
FAIR PRICE — your first 10 albums are free, then a one-time unlock. No subscription.

**Keywords:** photos,albums,organize,sort,trip,travel,library,screenshots,folders,map,world map,private
