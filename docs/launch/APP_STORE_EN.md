**Description:**

```
Thousands of photos and not a single album? Albumist finds your trips and turns them into real albums in your Photos app. You see everything first. No photo ever leaves your iPhone.

TRIPS BECOME ALBUMS
Albumist spots trips and day outings on its own, even ones from ten years ago, and keeps them apart from everyday life at home. Each gets an album with place and time, like "Lake Garda, August 2026". The albums live in Apple's Photos app, and with iCloud Photos on your Mac and iPad too.

LOOK FIRST, THEN CREATE
Before anything changes, Albumist shows you every album and its photos. Take photos out, rename, split or merge trips. Nothing happens until you tap "Create Albums Now".

EVERYTHING CAN BE UNDONE
A list shows exactly what was created, and "Undo" puts everything back. Albumist never deletes or edits your photos. Already have albums? Albumist adds to them instead of making duplicates.

[MAP] YOUR WORLD
A globe with every place you've taken photos. Replay a trip stop by stop with your best shots, and share your world as an image.
[NO MAP] YOUR TRAVEL ATLAS
A world map of every country you've photographed in, with numbers and flags. Ready to share.

SEARCH THE WAY YOU TALK
Type "dogs Italy 2023" and Albumist shows you the photos. Turn any results into an album.

MORE THAN TRIPS
• Countries, and cities if you like
• Places you visit often, named by you
• Screenshots by content: orders, tickets, recipes
• Documents like invoices and receipts
• Topics like dogs, beaches or food
• Months and years, if you like

PRIVATE ON YOUR IPHONE
• All analysis runs on your iPhone
• No account, no tracking, no ads
• Only for place names, iOS asks Apple, with the photo's location and never the photo. You can turn this off.
[MAP] • Map images come from Apple, only for the area you look at. Also optional.
• Made for VoiceOver, large text, Dark Mode and Reduce Motion

KEEPS GOING IN THE BACKGROUND
Big libraries take time. You can leave Albumist: as long as iOS allows, it keeps sorting and shows progress on your Lock Screen. Later, sort new photos with one tap or with Siri.

PAY ONCE, NO SUBSCRIPTION
Your first 10 albums are free. Then unlock Albumist once, no subscription.

Questions or ideas? Reach us through the support page. We read every message.
```

## Note on English locales

Enter the text as **English (U.S.)** and copy it to **English (U.K.)**. Some ASO guides say
Apple's German storefront also reads U.K. English keywords; Apple does not say so. Do not bend the
U.K. field for it; check App Analytics after launch.
