# Featuring Nomination (App Store Connect → Nominierungen)

**Typ:** Neue App (Start) · später: App-Erweiterung (Update 1.1)
**Zeitpunkt:** 6–12 Wochen vor dem Start, mindestens 3 Wochen.

**Beschreibung (Englisch, max. ~600 Zeichen):**
Albumist turns years of unsorted photos into real Apple Photos albums – trips with dates, countries and cities, screenshots and documents – entirely on device. It is built around trust: every change is previewed with a clear reason, existing albums are completed instead of duplicated, and everything can be undone. A shareable Travel Atlas lights up every country you've photographed in, and with Apple Intelligence it writes your personal photo story. Built natively for iOS 26 with SwiftUI, Vision, Foundation Models, App Intents and full accessibility.
