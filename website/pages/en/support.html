Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded?

No. All analysis runs on your iPhone. There is no server and no account. See the privacy policy for details.

Does the app delete or change my photos?

No. {{APP_NAME}} only creates albums and folders in the Photos app and adds photos to them. The photos themselves stay as they are. You can undo every change in the app.

Why does the first analysis take so long?

The first time, the app looks at your whole library. With many thousands of photos this can take a while. Trips and places come first. It’s fastest while your iPhone is charging. The app finishes the rest overnight in the background.

The app doesn’t see all my photos.

You probably shared only selected photos. Open Settings › Apps › {{APP_NAME}} › Photos and choose Full Access.

My photos are in iCloud. Does it work?

Yes. For trips and places, the information already on your iPhone is enough. Only for screenshots and documents can the app download originals from iCloud if you allow it, by default only on Wi-Fi. Videos are never downloaded. You can turn this off in the app’s settings.

A place or trip is wrong.

Places come from the GPS data in your photos. If a photo has none, the app can’t know where it was taken. You can reject or rename every suggestion before anything is created.

I bought the app. How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app’s settings and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you’re signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy it.

How do I get a refund?

App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

All of the app’s data stays on your iPhone. In the app’s settings, Rebuild analysis deletes the stored analysis. Deleting the app removes all of its data. Albums in the Photos app stay until you delete them yourself or undo them in the app.
